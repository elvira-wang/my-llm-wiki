With CHITUBOX v1.4.1, we're introducing ChituNest, a store for 3D printing resins and accessories from brands including Resione, JAMG HE, and Chitu Systems. Available right from the CHITUBOX home page, it launches with Black Friday deals and 25 CHITUBOX Dental licenses to give away.

Stock up on resin or pick up accessories at Black Friday prices. The campaign runs from ChituNest's launch through December 6, 2026, with promotional pricing available to both new and returning customers who purchase during the event.

Make a purchase on ChituNest during the campaign for a chance to win a CHITUBOX Dental license. We'll draw winners from campaign customers, with the following prizes:

Version 1.4.1 also adds the "Model Spacing Only" option in Copy, support for EvoDent E128 and E230 printers, and fixes for Free Cut, previews when copying supported models, and start-page rendering initialization.
