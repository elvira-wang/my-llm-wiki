# CHITUBOX v1.4.1: Meet ChituNest and Shop Black Friday Offers

In CHITUBOX v1.4.1, we're introducing ChituNest on the home page, making it easier to browse resins and accessories from brands including Resione, JAMG HE, and Chitu Systems. To celebrate, we're launching Black Friday offers alongside a CHITUBOX Dental license giveaway.

## Black Friday Prices, Starting at Launch

The campaign begins when ChituNest launches and runs through December 6, 2026. Promotional prices are available to both new and returning customers who purchase during the event. Whether you're replenishing resin or picking up accessories for your next project, explore the store's offers before you buy.

## Win a CHITUBOX Dental License

We'll draw winners from customers who make a purchase on ChituNest during the campaign, with 25 licenses to give away:

- 5 one-year licenses
- 5 six-month licenses
- 5 three-month licenses
- 10 one-month licenses

All prizes are for Dental, CHITUBOX's highest access tier. Your campaign purchase could bring you more than printing supplies: a chance to explore Dental access for up to a full year.

## Also New in v1.4.1

Copy now includes a "Model Spacing Only" option. Check it to apply the entered spacing between model bodies; leave it unchecked to calculate spacing between supports when the models have supports. We've also added EvoDent E128 and E230 printer support and fixed issues with Free Cut, previews when copying supported models, and start-page rendering initialization.
