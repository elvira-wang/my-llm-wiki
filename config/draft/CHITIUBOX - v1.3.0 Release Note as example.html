CHITUBOX V1.3.0 Change Log



Stay in the Model Flow

When you open CHITUBOX this week, everything may look familiar at first.
But as you spend more time with it, you'll notice that model handling has become much smoother.
Tasks that once required switching to other software can now be done directly within CHITUBOX,
giving you more freedom to organise models around your printing needs rather than their original structure.

Workflow Improvements

Shared Protection Case

Packaging multiple models together is now much easier. After adding supports, you can Combine multiple models and generate a single shared protection case for the entire set.

Model Reassembly

Bringing separated parts back together is now easier than ever. With Align, you can reassemble split models with precision, or combine only the parts you need based on your printing workflow.

More Flexible Model Splitting

Complex models can now be separated directly within the software, so there's no need to jump between applications.
With Free Cut, you can precisely customize cut surfaces and place alignment pins exactly where you need them.

Layout Refinements

Single and Dual Column Views

The layout workspace now supports both single（merge to right,merge to left) and dual column views, allowing you to choose the setup that best fits your workflow.
Single-column mode provides more room for model preparation and editing，and you can also switch freely between left- and right-aligned layouts.

Shortcut Enhancements



Expanded Shortcut Support

The detailed tool panel can be toggled directly from the keyboard, and pressing Alt displays or hides shortcut hints on demand.During model preparation, number keys can be used to switch layout modes and mirror directions more efficiently. When working with batch supports, shortcuts also make it easier to switch between support modes.

Faster Confirmations

Model editing now follows a more consistent keyboard-driven workflow.When exploring different options, you can use Enter to apply changes and Esc to cancel them, reducing the need to constantly move between the keyboard and mouse.

 

Keyboard Panning

Alongside middle-click panning, you can now use Shift + Arrow Keys to move the view with greater precision, helping you reach exactly the area you want to inspect.

More Flexible 3D View Navigation

View navigation is now more fluid, with keyboard shortcuts for smooth 3D rotation and flexible zoom controls.

Faster Axis Controls

Rotation shortcuts apply fixed angle increments, giving you greater precision and control when adjusting model orientation.

Import Settings Improvements

Smater Model Placement

Model duplication now includes two new placement options.“Centered on the Platform” automatically places duplicated models at the center of the build plate for more predictable placement.“Preserve Original World Coordinates” creates duplicates in their original positions, making it easy to preserve a backup before making further changes.

Tip

Quick Switching Between Support Actions

By coordinating the A, D, and E shortcut keys with your left hand, you can quickly and smoothly add, delete, and modify supports, making it especially suitable for users who prioritise efficiency.  

Additional Improvements

1、Batch workflows for dental users are now better supported. In ChituAction, you can preset up to three processes with different parameters and switch between them whenever needed.

2、Slice files now support more printers, and exported goo and prz files can display model thumbnails as well, making it much easier to identify the right model among large numbers of sliced files.

3、As you adjust a model, model volume and estimated print price are now displayed in real time in the bottom-right corner, helping you stay informed throughout the printing process.

4、Choosing materials and adjusting print settings now flows more naturally. Open the ChituRMA directly from the model workspace, and clear unused print presets in bulk to keep your workflow organized.
