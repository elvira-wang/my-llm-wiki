CHITUBOX 1.4.0: From splitting models to stacking prints

Your model comes in one piece. That doesn't mean you have to print it that way.

Some parts are just easier to paint separately.

Other times, you simply want to pack a few more small parts into a single go.

CHITUBOX 1.4.0 introduces finer, more intuitive ways to split your workflow. Take a closer peek.

Some parts are easier to paint on their own

The new Part tool starts by automatically dividing your model into color-coded regions. Adjust those regions to suit your needs, then split the model along their boundaries.

Low-quality models can cause cuts to fail or leave broken surfaces.

Give your artwork a little depth

Want to carve your own logo on a model, make a stamp, or turn an image into a relief? You can now add an image label. Play with the parameters to fine-tune your outcome.

Use high-contrast images and clean backgrounds to make details pop.

No room left? Think up.

Running out of build plate space for your miniature squad? Just stack them. Enable Copy in Z direction, and CHITUBOX will handle the rest.

Stacked printing isn't recommended for large models.

More than one cut. All in one go.

Taking the handle off a cup? A single cut won't quite do it. Free cut now lets you draw multiple surfaces at once.

Cutting results depend on model quality.

One overhang point at a time. At your own pace.

Overhang points now stay visible instead of disappearing after supports being added. The view no longer jumps back to the first untreated island.

Want automatic support to skip an island? Mark it manually. Find them with new filter whenever you like.

A few more things

The start page now has an autosave tab, with thumbnails to help you recognize your saved projects. In settings, you can choose where autosaves go, how often they're created, how many to keep, and how long to keep them.

You can also choose which cavities to fill, switch between global and local coordinate systems, or hold Alt while dragging a model to make a copy.

The new global toolbar brings together tools for selection and measurement, with lasso and brush selection options. And if your computer has more than one graphics card, you can choose which one CHITUBOX uses in settings.

We've also fixed lag when moving supported models during 3D collision detection, along with an issue that caused pixels to go missing during slicing at pixel sizes of 0.025 mm or smaller.
